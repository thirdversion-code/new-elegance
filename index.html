Help & Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find prompt answers about our appointments, bridal consultations, boutique products, and salon policies.

1 What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We understand that schedules can change. We kindly request at least 24 hours' notice for regular salon services (nails, facials, hair spa) and 72 hours' notice for bridal packages or multi-hour sessions.

Rescheduling within these windows is completely complimentary. Simply send a quick message to our front desk via WhatsApp with your updated preferred date.

2 How do bridal consultations and look trials work?

Every bride is unique. Our bridal consultation includes a thorough skin texture evaluation, wedding lehenga/outfit undertone analysis, jewelry coordination, and discussion between Airbrush and HD bridal finishes.

We recommend scheduling your trial 2 to 4 months before your wedding. If you decide to confirm your complete Royal Bridal package with us, your trial fee is 100% credited toward your final balance.

3 What hygiene and sanitation standards does your salon follow?

Client hygiene is our highest priority. All stainless steel nail clippers, cuticle pushers, and styling tools undergo hospital-grade ultrasonic cleansing followed by high-temperature UV sterilization pouches opened right in front of you.

We use fresh disposable biodegradable towels, sanitized makeup brush sets, and sealed sterile vortex tips for all HydraFacials to ensure a 100% germ-free, pampered experience.

4 How long do nail extensions last and how should I care for them?

Our gel and acrylic extensions typically last 3 to 4 weeks before needing an infill, depending on your natural nail growth rate.

To maintain their mirror shine and prevent lifting: avoid using your nails to pry open containers, wear protective gloves when handling strong cleaning chemicals, and apply cuticle oil daily before bed.

5 Are facial treatments safe for acne-prone or highly sensitive skin?

Yes. Before commencing any skin treatment, our licensed estheticians perform a complimentary patch check and skin diagnosis. Our Medical-Grade HydraFacial features adjustable vortex vacuum levels and hypoallergenic saline-peptide infusions.

We do not use synthetic parabens, artificial fragrances, or aggressive bleaching agents on sensitive complexions.

6 Do you travel for destination weddings or on-site venue appointments?

Yes, our master bridal makeup artists and mehndi team regularly travel for destination weddings, luxury resorts, and hotel suites.

Destination packages include dedicated lead artists, junior assistants for family/bridesmaid looks, ring lights, and emergency vanity kits. Reach out with your wedding destination and dates for a bespoke quotation.
